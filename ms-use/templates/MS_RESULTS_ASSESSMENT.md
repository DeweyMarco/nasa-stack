## 1. Purpose

This document scores the six Appendix E Results Assessment factors for a specific use of [PROJECT NAME]. These scores reflect how credibly this M&S was applied for a particular decision. The scores must appear in all results reports (M&S 35).

## 2. Scoring Scale

- **0 — Not performed**
- **1 — Informally performed:** Done but not documented
- **2 — Planned and tracked:** Documented with some gaps
- **3 — Well-defined:** Complete and consistently applied
- **4 — Quantitatively controlled:** Measured, metrics-driven
